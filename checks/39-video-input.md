## Corrections and open questions

- **Almost nothing in this unit is ossia-specific and almost none of it has been
  verified here.** The limited-range video claim, the latency measurement
  method, the auto-exposure warning, and the frame-rate mismatch are general
  video facts, correct as far as they go, stated from experience rather than
  from measurement in 3.8.2.
- The motion-detection section describes a technique and ships no shader for it.
  It would be a good addition to `library/shaders/39/`, and the unit currently
  reads as though one exists somewhere.
