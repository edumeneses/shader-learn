## Design notes

The milestone grades the course's central convention explicitly: requirement 3
is that every control is role-named, and the brief says P4 is where that is
graded.

**Requirement 8, the written page, is the one most likely to be skipped**, which
is why it is a requirement rather than advice and why the success criteria
repeat it.

The "what to expose and what to hide" section is the design content. The test it
gives, whether you would reach for a control while looking at the projection
rather than at the controller, is the useful form of the question.
