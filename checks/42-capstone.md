# checks/42-capstone

**Unit 42, the capstone.** Pinned to *ossia score* 3.8.2.

## Shaders

None. The reader's piece is the deliverable.

## Design notes

Three shapes are offered, a fixed work, a performable piece, and an instrument,
and they are graded differently. That is deliberate: a course that only accepted
a performance would exclude the people who came for the print, and the
discipline being taught, finishing and documenting, is the same in all three.

**The documentation section is the real deliverable** and the unit says so. Its
"what breaks and what to do" subsection is described as the section that gets
read at eleven at night, which is the honest argument for writing it.

The closing section states plainly that the most common failure among people who
get good at this is that the technique becomes the subject. That is a position,
and it is the one the whole course has implied.
